SEARULEA READY-TO-FILL TEMPLATE — DESIGN PLAN

Communication job
By the end, a SEARULEA client or team member should be able to assemble a polished on-brand presentation quickly by duplicating the closest page variant and replacing clearly marked text, image and data fields.

Format
- 16:9, 1440 × 810 canvas.
- Lora for presentation-defining titles and key numbers.
- DM Sans for labels, body copy, captions and data.
- Brand palette: Deep Sea, Tide, Lagoon, Sea Foam, Shore, Pearl and controlled Amber.

Library structure
- Authoring guide and page index.
- Four covers.
- Three agendas.
- Four section dividers.
- Five narrative structures.
- Six image-led structures.
- Four metric structures.
- Five process and timeline structures.
- Six editable chart structures.
- Four comparison structures.
- Three editable table structures.
- Four profile and team structures.
- Four proof, gallery and location structures.
- Three closing structures.

Authoring convention
- Bracketed copy marks fields to replace.
- Image frames carry a small REPLACE IMAGE label.
- Native charts and tables keep neutral example data and are editable through PowerPoint's Edit Data controls.
- Speaker notes explain the intended use and cite user-provided brand assets.
