Brand Storytelling The full reference — how SEARULEA speaks

Lead with vision. Prove with facts.

This chapter carries the complete storytelling document. It defines how the brand communicates, with which words, and to whom — a working tool for everyone who writes on behalf of SEARULEA, to ensure consistency, precision and impact in every communication.

S.1

The Register

SEARULEA speaks like someone who knows their subject inside out — and has no need to prove it.

A voice that leads with vision, proves with facts. International in its references, Moroccan without apology, technical without being cold. Like its founder, SEARULEA communicates from a place of conviction: it states its ambitions clearly, relies on facts and allows results to speak for themselves.

Integration · Sovereignty · Sustainability
S.2

The Style — five rules

01

Lead with the point

Then build the argument. A short sentence comes first — it lands the idea. A longer sentence follows with context and depth.

02

Facts persuade

Numbers, results, locations and proper nouns carry credibility. Never surround them with qualifiers or superlatives.

03

Present tense

When something is true, state it. Prefer conviction over conditional language.

04

Conviction over emotion

Humanity comes from the strength of convictions — never from emotional claims or generic statements.

05

Be precise

Every sentence contributes information, perspective or evidence. If a word does not add value, remove it.

S.3

Do — name facts, demonstrate expertise

Lead with the vision. Anchor it with proof. Start with where SEARULEA is going; follow with the facts, figures and commitments that make the ambition tangible.

Name the facts. Numbers, locations, certifications, partners, milestones. 375 hectares. Ras El Ma. 2,790 tonnes by 2031.

Own Moroccan pride without justifying it. "Rooted in Morocco by conviction." The vision creates momentum; the proof creates trust.

Treat social commitments as operational commitments. Speak about inclusion and training with the same precision as infrastructure. "40% women across the workforce."

Compress complexity. From our sea to your port. Six words — one integrated value chain.

Think systems, not production. SEARULEA is not a fish farm — it is the infrastructure of Moroccan aquaculture. Our hatchery supplies the next generation of North African aquaculture.

S.4

Don't — never use these patterns

No generic product language. Every environmental commitment is backed by measurable actions, targets or infrastructure.

No unsupported leadership claims. "First in Morocco" only matters when connected to a larger ambition, a new model or a regional impact. And never stack adjectives.

No borrowed voices. SEARULEA does not sound like Nordic or American brands. Moroccan by conviction, international by ambition.

Never apologise. Not for ambition, not for being Moroccan. Never soften the scale of the vision — state it clearly, then prove it.

S.5

Key messages — Clients & Distributors

IoT traceability

Every batch is traceable through an integrated IoT system. Certification follows the fish at every stage, from our sea to your port. One point of contact, complete documentation.

ONSSA certified

All products are certified by ONSSA and produced to European standards. Sea bream, seabass and meagre raised in the Mediterranean, exported through Ras Kebdana.

Integrated value chain

From offshore farming to fresh processing and export logistics, every stage is managed within a single operational model — documented roadmap, scalable volumes, partnerships from Phase 1.

"Searulea is built to last. Certified offshore production, batch-by-batch traceability, biosafety and solar energy integrated into the CAPEX from day one. Volumes that grow alongside our partners."To clients — and never on price: the objective is not to be the cheapest supplier, but the most trusted one.
S.6

Key messages — Press

Built to last

Most industries are built first and corrected later. Biosafety, solar energy, IoT traceability and 40% women across the workforce are part of the CAPEX — not additions made to satisfy ESG requirements.

Moroccan sovereignty

Morocco fishes; Morocco exports canned sardines. Yet juveniles are imported and feed is sourced abroad, in an industry 3,500 km of coastline position Morocco to lead. Searulea changes that. Structurally.

A regional blueprint

What is built in the Oriental Region today will be observed across North Africa tomorrow — an African operator meeting international export standards while building critical infrastructure locally.

"Searulea does not only produce fish. It builds Morocco's capacity to produce, transform and scale aquaculture on its own terms."
S.7

Key messages — Investors

An underserved market

European demand grows while supply remains structurally constrained. Searulea enters with a 375-hectare concession secured and commercial partnerships established ahead of production.

A scalable model

The value lies in the integration of the entire chain — production, processing, export. Growth is structured through documented phases to 2,790 tonnes by 2031.

Critical infrastructure

Beyond the production asset: hatchery and feed capabilities that support the development of the wider North African aquaculture sector.

S.8

Vocabulary — words to use

Rigour

Precision without excess — the discipline and technical standards under every decision.

Biosafety

Not a marketing claim — an operational reality integrated into the CAPEX from day one.

Infrastructure

Positions SEARULEA as a systemic actor shaping an industry, not a standalone producer.

Vertical integration

Every stage of the value chain — production, biosafety, cold chain, export logistics.

Traceability

Every batch, every stage — a measurable commitment supported by systems and documentation.

Standards

A benchmark, not an aspiration — European, export and operational standards define how SEARULEA operates.

Scalability

Growth designed from the outset, through documented phases and infrastructure built for expansion.

Sovereignty

The ability to produce, transform and create value locally — a strategic objective.

S.9

Signature expressions & phrases to avoid

The tagline

From our sea to your port.
The promise of integration in a single line — and the only line that appears on assets.

Editorial only

Rooted in Morocco. Built for the world. Moroccan roots as foundation, never as justification. The aquaculture of tomorrow. Built today. The model others will replicate.

Avoid

"Passion for the sea" — passion does not certify operations. "Sustainable development" as a standalone claim. "We believe in…" — belief is not evidence. Prefer: we build, we operate, we document, we certify, we scale.

S.10

The Manifesto

Morocco has 3,500 kilometres of coastline and direct access to European markets. SEARULEA is building the infrastructure to unlock their full potential.

In Ras El Ma, on a 375-hectare offshore concession, we are developing an integrated aquaculture value chain designed to operate as a single system: offshore farming, fresh processing at port, refrigerated export logistics and end-to-end traceability.

One stage supports the next.

Our approach is defined as much by infrastructure as by the standards embedded within it. Biosafety from day one. Solar energy integrated into the project. Local employment and training anchored in the region. Decisions built into the CAPEX, not initiatives added later.

Over time, our hatchery and feed operations are designed to support the wider development of aquaculture across North Africa — infrastructure designed to meet international standards from day one, scale with the needs of the industry, and contribute to the long-term development of North African aquaculture.
