## Introduction

This document lays the foundations of Searulea's voice. It defines how the brand communicates, with which words, and to whom. It is a working tool for everyone who writes on behalf of Searulea — to ensure consistency, precision and impact in every communication.

## 01 — Tone of Voice: The Register

Searulea speaks like someone who knows their subject inside out and has no need to prove it. A voice that leads with vision, proves with facts. International in its references, Moroccan without apology, technical without being cold.

INTEGRATION · SOVEREIGNTY · SUSTAINABILITY

> "It would not try to impress — it would simply demonstrate competence and let that speak."
> — Abdellah Mansouri

## 02 — Tone of Voice: The Style

**A voice that demonstrates, not declares.** Like its founder, Abdellah Mansouri, SEARULEA communicates from a place of conviction. The company states its ambitions clearly, relies on facts and allows results to speak for themselves.

- **Lead with the point. Then build the argument.** A short sentence comes first. It lands the idea. A longer sentence follows. It provides context and depth.
- **Facts persuade.** Numbers, results, locations and proper nouns carry credibility. Avoid surrounding them with unnecessary qualifiers or superlatives.
- **Write in the present tense.** When something is true, state it. Prefer conviction over conditional language.
- **Conviction over emotion.** Searulea communicates through clarity, expertise and belief. Humanity comes from the strength of its convictions, not from emotional claims or generic statements.
- **Be precise.** Every sentence should contribute information, perspective or evidence. If a word does not add value, remove it.

## 03 — Tone of Voice: Do & Don't

### DO: Name facts. Demonstrate expertise.

- **Lead with the vision. Anchor it with proof.** Start with where Searulea is going. Follow with facts, figures and commitments that make the ambition tangible.
- **Name the facts.** Use numbers, locations, certifications, partners and milestones. They carry credibility more effectively than adjectives. *375 hectares. Ras El Ma. 2,790 tonnes by 2031.*
- **Begin with the ambition. End with the evidence.** The vision creates momentum. The proof creates trust. Own Moroccan pride without justifying it. *"Rooted in Morocco by conviction."*
- **Treat social commitments as operational commitments.** Speak about inclusion, training and local development with the same precision used to discuss infrastructure, production or investment. *"40% women across the workforce."*
- **Use concise expressions to communicate complex ideas.** *From our sea to your port.* Six words. One integrated value chain.
- **Think beyond production. Think systems.** Searulea is not a fish farm. It is the infrastructure of Moroccan aquaculture. *Our hatchery supplies the next generation of North African aquaculture, not only our own operations.*

### DON'T: Never use these patterns.

- **Avoid generic product language.** Every environmental commitment should be backed by measurable actions, targets or infrastructure.
- **Avoid unsupported leadership claims.** *First in Morocco* is only meaningful when connected to a larger ambition, a new model or a regional impact.
- **Avoid stacking adjectives.**
- **Avoid borrowing the voice of Nordic or American brands.** SEARULEA has its own register. Moroccan by conviction. International by ambition.
- **Never apologise for ambition. Never apologise for being Moroccan.** Never soften the scale of the vision. State it clearly. Then prove it.

## Key Messages

### Clients & Distributors

- **IoT Traceability** — Every batch is traceable through an integrated IoT system. Certification follows the fish at every stage, from our sea to your port. One point of contact. Complete documentation.
- **ONSSA Certified** — All products are certified by ONSSA and produced in accordance with European standards. Sea bream, seabass and meagre raised in the Mediterranean and exported through Ras Kebdana.
- **Integrated Value Chain** — From offshore farming to fresh processing and export logistics, every stage is managed within a single operational model. A documented roadmap, scalable volumes and long-term partnerships from Phase 1.

> "Searulea is built to last. Certified offshore production, batch-by-batch traceability, biosafety and solar energy integrated into the CAPEX from day one. One point of contact. Complete documentation. Volumes that grow alongside our partners."

**We never compete on price.** Price may be competitive. It is never the message. We talk about reliability, traceability, operational control, documentation, biosafety and long-term scalability. The objective is not to be the cheapest supplier. The objective is to become the most trusted one.

### Press

- **Built to Last** — Most industries are built first and corrected later. Searulea takes a different approach. Biosafety, solar energy, IoT traceability and a target of 40% women across the workforce are integrated into the project from day one. They are part of the CAPEX, not additions made later to satisfy ESG requirements.
- **Moroccan Sovereignty** — A country is sovereign over a resource when it can produce it, transform it and create value from it. Morocco fishes. Morocco exports canned sardines. Yet fish juveniles are imported, feed is sourced abroad, and foreign operators continue to structure an industry that Morocco's 3,500 kilometres of coastline naturally position it to lead. Searulea changes that. Structurally.
- **A Regional Blueprint** — What is being built in Nador today will be observed across North Africa tomorrow. Searulea demonstrates that an African operator can meet international export standards while building critical infrastructure locally. The ambition extends beyond a single farm. It is a model for the region.

> "Searulea does not only produce fish. It builds Morocco's capacity to produce, transform and scale aquaculture on its own terms."

### Investors

- **An Underserved Market** — European demand continues to grow. Supply remains structurally constrained. Searulea enters the market with a 375-hectare government concession already secured and commercial partnerships established ahead of production.
- **A Scalable Model** — The value is not limited to offshore farming. It lies in the integration of the entire value chain, from production to processing and export. Growth is structured through documented phases, with a target capacity of 2,790 tonnes by 2031.
- **Critical Infrastructure** — Searulea is building more than a production asset. The long-term ambition includes hatchery and feed capabilities, creating infrastructure that supports the development of the wider North African aquaculture sector.

> "Searulea is building the future of Mediterranean aquaculture. The project combines a 375-hectare government concession, a fully integrated operating model and commercial partnerships established before launch. Production scales through documented phases to reach 2,790 tonnes by 2031. The ambition extends beyond farming. Searulea is building infrastructure designed to strengthen the long-term competitiveness of North African aquaculture."

## Vocabulary — Words to Use

- **Rigour** — Precision without excess. The discipline, operational control and technical standards that underpin every decision.
- **Biosafety** — A technical term that immediately establishes credibility. Not a marketing claim, but an operational reality integrated into the CAPEX from day one.
- **Infrastructure** — A perspective that extends beyond the farm itself. The word positions Searulea as a systemic actor helping shape an industry, rather than a standalone producer.
- **Vertical Integration** — From hatchery to fresh processing. Searulea controls every stage of the value chain, from production and biosafety to cold chain management and export logistics.
- **Traceability** — Every batch. Every stage. A measurable commitment supported by systems, documentation and operational discipline.
- **Standards** — A benchmark, not an aspiration. European standards, export standards and operational standards define how Searulea is built and how it operates.
- **Scalability** — Growth designed from the outset. Production volumes increase through documented phases, supported by infrastructure built for expansion.
- **Sovereignty** — The ability to produce, transform and create value locally. A strategic objective supported by infrastructure, expertise and long-term investment.

## Signature Expressions

- **From our sea to your port.** — The promise of integration in a single line. It communicates end-to-end control of the value chain and speaks directly to buyers looking for a reliable export partner, from production to delivery.
- **Rooted in Morocco. Built for the world.** — An expression that reframes the narrative. Moroccan roots are not presented as a limitation or a differentiator to justify. They are the foundation from which Searulea builds an internationally relevant business.
- **The aquaculture of tomorrow. Built today.** — Searulea is not built around today's standards. It anticipates what the industry is becoming: integrated, traceable, biosafe, export-ready and sovereign. The ambition is to build a model others will look to replicate.

## Phrases to Avoid

- **"Passion for the sea"** — Passion does not certify operations, secure production or build trust. Prefer: *Mastery of the aquaculture value chain. Integrated offshore production. End-to-end operational control.*
- **"Sustainable development"** (as a standalone claim) — Without measurable commitments, sustainability remains an abstract concept. At Searulea, sustainability is built into the infrastructure itself. Prefer: *Integrated biosafety. Solar energy embedded in the CAPEX. 40% women across the workforce. IoT traceability by batch.*
- **"We believe in..."** — Belief is not evidence. Searulea is defined by what it builds, operates and documents. Prefer: *We build... We operate... We document... We certify... We scale...*

## Manifesto

Morocco has 3,500 kilometres of coastline and direct access to European markets. SEARULEA is building the infrastructure to unlock their full potential.

In Ras El Ma, on a 375-hectare offshore concession, we are developing an integrated aquaculture value chain designed to operate as a single system: offshore farming, fresh processing at port, refrigerated export logistics and end-to-end traceability.

One stage supports the next.

Our approach is defined as much by infrastructure as by the standards embedded within it. Biosafety from day one. Solar energy integrated into the project. Local employment and training anchored in the region. Decisions built into the CAPEX, not initiatives added later.

Over time, our hatchery and feed operations are designed to support the wider development of aquaculture across North Africa.

SEARULEA is building aquaculture infrastructure designed to meet international standards from day one, scale with the needs of the industry and contribute to the long-term development of North African aquaculture.
